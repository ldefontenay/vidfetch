VidFetch is an accessible way into YouTube on Windows: search, listen, follow channels without an account, read comments and transcripts, and download what you want to keep, as sound or video. It is built for screen reader users first, and works fully by keyboard. It can also download from many other video sites.

This is the first public release, a beta. Please say what goes wrong, on the [issues page](https://github.com/ldefontenay/vidfetch/issues).

## Download

- **vidfetch.exe**: the program itself. Nothing to install: put it in a folder of its own and run it.
- **vidfetch.zip**: the same, zipped, for when a browser or email will not pass an exe.

Windows SmartScreen may warn that the app is unrecognised, because it is not yet signed. Choose "More info", then "Run anyway". The first time you listen or download, VidFetch offers to fetch [yt-dlp](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp), the free program it uses to reach the videos, from its own page.

## What it does

- Searches YouTube, with filters for channels, playlists, length and when videos were uploaded, and plays a video's sound straight away, in a Play window with simple keys for moving, speed, volume and chapters.
- Follows channels with no Google account, lists what is new from them, and brings in your YouTube subscriptions from Google Takeout, or your channels from FreeTube or NewPipe.
- Lists a video's related videos, its comments and its whole transcript, each a keystroke away. Copy all text puts a transcript on the clipboard, ready for an AI summary.
- Downloads videos and whole playlists as M4A or MP3 sound or as MP4 video, with captions if you like. A playlist gets a folder of its own, and updating it fetches only what is new.
- Keeps a list of your downloads, in categories, and offers a file you deleted by mistake again.
- Speaks what it is doing through your screen reader. Press F1 for [the guide](https://ldefontenay.github.io/vidfetch/userguide/), with tutorials.

## Tested with

- JAWS on Windows 11, through seven rounds of testing by its developer, who is blind.
- NVDA and Narrator, in one round.
