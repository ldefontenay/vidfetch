VidFetch - milestone 4: search, channels, categories, and listening
===================================================================

Why: your answers to log entry 3. Log entry 4 says what was built, what I tried, and has a few
new questions.

How to start it: run
C:\Users\ldefo\dev\VidFetch\target\release\vidfetch.exe

Before you start, a warning: the first time this version starts, it moves your downloads into a
folder called Uncategorised inside your output folder, as you agreed in log entry 3, Q6. Nothing is
deleted, and File Explorer shows the same. Settings, the queue and what VidFetch remembers about
your downloads follow them.

Some steps need the network. Listening online takes about 5 seconds to start; that is expected.


Section 1 - categories
----------------------

1.1  Start VidFetch. Is it said that your downloads were moved into the category Uncategorised?
ANSWER: Yes

1.2  Press Alt+C. Is it the "Category to show" box, on Uncategorised or All categories? Press the
     Down and Up arrows: does the list change to show each, and is it said what is shown?
ANSWER: Yes

1.3  Press Alt+G for the Category menu, then N for New category. Type Music and press Enter. Is it
     said that Music was made and is now shown, with an empty list?
ANSWER: Yes.

1.4  Go back to Uncategorised with Alt+C. Choose "Me at the zoo" in the list and press
     Ctrl+Shift+M. Does a short menu of categories open? Choose Music. Is it said that it moved?
     Show Music with Alt+C: is it there?
ANSWER: Yes.

1.5  With Music shown, press Ctrl+N. Tab to the Category box (or Alt+G): is Music already chosen?
     Press Escape.
ANSWER: Yes

1.6  Alt+G, R, rename Music to Songs. Is it said? Then Alt+G, D, Delete the category shown: are you
     asked first, with keeping it as the safe answer? Answer to delete it. Is it said that "Me at
     the zoo" went back into Uncategorised, with nothing deleted?
ANSWER: Yes, works perfectly.

Section 2 - the Play window
---------------------------

2.1  Show All categories, choose a long download, such as one of the Blender films, and press
     Enter. Let it play for 30 seconds or so, then press Escape. Press Enter on it again: is it said
     "Carrying on from" the time where you stopped?
ANSWER: Yes, very good. Note that while the focus is on the "where playing is" field, Jaws doesn't see that its updating. Ideally, it should be able to update silently. If that can't be done, it just needs to be documented and the user needs to know that they need to tab and shift-tab to get the correct time while the audio is playing.

2.2  Press the Up and Down arrows: louder and quieter, with "Volume 70 percent" or the like said?
ANSWER: Yes, very good. Please make volume adjustable in increments of 5%.

2.3  Press Shift+Up two or three times: faster each time, with the speed said, and the voice not
     higher? Shift+Down back to normal speed. Is a speed between 1.25 and 2 pleasant to listen to?
ANSWER: Works. Some small artifacts seam to creep in at 2x, not sure if much can be done about that. Sound quite distorted at the slowest speeds. If there are good existing dsp crates, and not too hard to implement, this could be a nice touch, but its a bonus and not a deal-breaker.

2.4  Press End: does it go to 10 seconds before the end? Press T: is where it is said?
ANSWER: Yes.

2.5  Press Page Down: does the next download in the list start? Page Up: the previous one? Press
     Escape.
ANSWER: Page up and down work well. One odity, Jaws announces the title of the current video and then the title of the next one. Ideally it would only announce the title of the next video to play.

2.6  Alt+O for the Options menu: are "Playing speed" and "Play the next one at the end" there?
     Tick "Play the next one at the end", then play a short download to its end: does the next
     start by itself? Untick it afterwards if you prefer.
ANSWER: Yes, works nicely.
