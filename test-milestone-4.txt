VidFetch - milestone 4: search, channels, categories, and listening
===================================================================

Why: your answers to log entry 3. Log entry 4 says what was built, what I tried, and has a few
new questions.

How to start it: run
C:\Users\ldefo\dev\VidFetch\target\release\vidfetch.exe

Before you start, a warning: the first time this version starts, it moves your downloads into a
folder called Uncategorised inside your output folder, as you agreed in log entry 3, Q6. Nothing is
deleted, and File Explorer shows the same. Settings, the queue and what VidFetch remembers about
your downloads follow them.

Some steps need the network. Listening online takes about 5 seconds to start; that is expected.

----------------------

ANSWER:

ANSWER:

2.2  Press the Up and Down arrows: louder and quieter, with "Volume 70 percent" or the like said?
ANSWER:

     higher? Shift+Down back to normal speed. Is a speed between 1.25 and 2 pleasant to listen to?

2.5  Press Page Down: does the next download in the list start? Page Up: the previous one? Press

     what was found, each with its channel, length and age, and is the number found said?

     is it said how many?

     Press Page Down: does the next one in the results play? Press Escape.
