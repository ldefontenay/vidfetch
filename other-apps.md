# Other YouTube programs, and where to get them

You asked where to get the programs mentioned in log entry 7. Each address below answered when I
checked it on 9 November 2026. I have not installed or tried any of them, so what they do comes from
their own pages.

## For Windows, made for screen reader users

- **A11YTube**: https://github.com/Daoductrung/A11YTube
- **Accessible YouTube Downloader Pro**, also known as HexPlayer.
  - Its code and releases: https://github.com/sulaiman-alqusaimi/accessible_youtube_downloader_pro
  - Its page on Blind Help: https://blindhelp.net/software/accessible-youtube-downloader-pro
  - Its page on Tecwindow: https://tecwindow.net/en/windows/download-files/accessible-youtube-downloader-pro/

## Add-ons for NVDA

These work inside NVDA, so they are no use under JAWS.

- **YoutubePlus**: https://github.com/NVDATH/Youtube-Plus-for-NVDA/
- **youtubeDownloader**: https://github.com/beyondsighttech/nvda-youtube-downloader/

## Private YouTube programs, not made especially for screen readers

- **FreeTube**, for Windows, Mac and Linux: https://freetubeapp.io/
- **NewPipe**, for Android phones: https://newpipe.net/
  - On F-Droid: https://f-droid.org/en/packages/org.schabi.newpipe/

On a GitHub page, the program itself is usually under the link called Releases.
