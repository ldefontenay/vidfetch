========================================================================

Why: your answers to test-milestone-4 and log entry 4. Log entry 5 says what changed, what I
tried, and has five new questions.

How to start it: run
C:\Users\ldefo\dev\VidFetch\target\release\vidfetch.exe

Some steps need the network. Until VidFetch next updates yt-dlp, listening online still takes about
5 seconds to start; section 6 brings in the faster yt-dlp.
