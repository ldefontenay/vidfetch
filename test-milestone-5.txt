VidFetch - milestone 5: the YouTube window, playlists, and faster starts
========================================================================

Why: your answers to test-milestone-4 and log entry 4. Log entry 5 says what changed, what I
tried, and has five new questions.

How to start it: run
C:\Users\ldefo\dev\VidFetch\target\release\vidfetch.exe

Some steps need the network. Until VidFetch next updates yt-dlp, listening online still takes about
5 seconds to start; section 6 brings in the faster yt-dlp.

-----------------------------------------

     "Show queue" and "Show video in file explorer"?

1.3  Choose a single video in the list, then open the Download menu. Are "Back" and "Update

1.4  With nothing downloading, is "Stop download" greyed out? Now press the Update playlist button

------------------------------

     results for Blender open movie", with the results in its list?

2.2  Tab through it. Do you hear the list, then Play, Download, Search and Close window, then

ANSWER: Yes, very good.

ANSWER: Yes

3.7  Choose a video from a channel you do not follow, and press Alt+V for the Video menu. Is
     "Unfollow channel" greyed out, and "Follow channel" available? Press Escape.

Section 4 - the Play window
---------------------------

4.1  In the YouTube window, press Enter on a video. Is "Please wait" said, and nothing else until
     it plays?
ANSWER: It says please wait, and then announces the player window's controls, explaining what the arrows do etc. This is not necessary. It would be good if these controls weren't spoken.

4.2  Tab to "Where playing is" and leave it there. Does JAWS read the time as it changes, or not?
     Is that pleasant, or does it chatter? (Log entry 5, Q1.)
ANSWER: Jaws behaves exactly as I would want it to behave, i.e. it doesn't constantly read the time out, but when I query the time with the say-line command, the current time is spoken and that eliminates confusion. Good as is.

4.3  Press Up and Down. Does the volume move in steps of 5%?
ANSWER: Yes.

4.4  Press Page Down. Is only the next title said, not the current one first? Does the next video
     start sooner than it did in milestone 4?
ANSWER: Page up and page down behave exactly right. The videos still take about 5 seconds to load. Not a big problem, but would be nice if it could be a bit smoother.

4.5  Press Shift+Up until twice the speed, then Shift+Down to the slowest. Is the sound clearer
     than before, at both ends?
ANSWER: Yes.

4.6  Close the Play window. In the Options menu, choose "Cut silences when playing". Play a video
     with pauses in it. Are the pauses cut down to about a second? Choose it again to turn it off.
ANSWER: Yes, appears to work.

4.7  Search for 1g4rGEA4g-U and press Enter on it, the video that played no sound last time. Does
     it play now? It may take a few seconds longer the first time, as VidFetch fetches its sound
     whole.
ANSWER: It played, but took about two minutes. Is this expected? It would be good to set expectations for the user in the documentation, explaining what this type of video is and why the different approach is needed.
I pressed ctrl+d to download it from the player window, and that worked very quicly, presumably because the sound had already been fetched.

ANYTHING ODD:
No.

     nothing selected. Is it said that all the text was copied? Paste it in Notepad: is it all
     there?
ANSWER: Jaws announces "nothing selected" and nothing is copied to the clipboard.

5.2  In the Options menu, choose "Offer copied web addresses". In your browser, copy the address of

     it? Press Not now. Turn the option off again if you prefer it off.
ANSWER: It works. I would also like the option to just stream the sound in the player instead of downloading it. This option to stream the sound instead of downloading should also be available when pressing ctrl+n.

ANYTHING ODD:
Nothing odd, very pleased.

Notes:
The initial idea with this app was to create a very functional Youtube downloader. It has now evolved to something far more useful, i.e. an accessible interface for Youtube. I'm very pleased with it.
Pleas adapt documentation and also anything in the app to reflect that its an accessible interface for Youtube which can act as a downloader for other video sites.
With this in mind, please research any further features, enhancements or refinements that would be helpful to include before a first beta release.
