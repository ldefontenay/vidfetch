Section 2 - the Play window
---------------------------

2.1  Press Enter on a download. Are the explanations of the keys no longer read as the window opens?
ANSWER: Yes. Could we have the player start with absolutely nothing announced by jaws? Just "please wait" when streaming?

2.2  Press Alt+K. Are you in a Keys box, and can you read it line by line with the arrow keys?
ANSWER: Yes. Please modify the message as follows:
Left and Right arrows: move 2 seconds back or forward.
Home: start of video. End: move to 10 seconds before the end.
T: time elapsed.
Up and Down arrows: adjust volume.
Shift with Up and Down arrows: adjust speed.
Page Down and Page Up: skip to next or previous video in the list.
Ctrl Page Down and Page Up: jump to next or previous chapter.
Ctrl+D: download video, when listening online.
Tab out of this help box to make sure these keys work reliably.
Escape: close.

2.3  Press Alt+P to go back to Play or pause. Do the Left and Right arrows still move through the
     sound? Press Escape.
ANSWER: Yes. Note that alt+p does activate the play/pause button, but does not put focus there. That's ok. It also means that arrow keys and pgdn and pgup don't work in a message box, which is normal.

No.
